About NEXUS ORBITAL

What is NEXUS ORBITAL?

An advanced intelligence platform for tracking near-Earth asteroids, estimating resource value, and computing optimal mission trajectories for asteroid mining operations.

How to Use

  1. Browse the NEO table. Use search and filters. Click column headers to sort.
  2. Click any asteroid row to select it — data populates the Mission Planner.
  3. Set a departure window and max flight time, then click Compute Optimal Missions.
  4. Review ranked options. Click Select This Mission for a full mission dashboard.
  5. Click View Trajectory to render the arc on the 3D globe.
  6. Use My Missions to save and manage your mission plans.

Key Terms

Δv (Delta-v)
Total velocity change required. Lower = cheaper. km/s.
AU (Astronomical Unit)
Earth–Sun distance ≈ 150 million km.
Potentially Hazardous
NASA designation: >140m diameter, orbit within 0.05 AU of Earth.
ROI
Return on Investment — net profit as percentage of total cost.
Lambert's Problem
Orbital mechanics calculation finding the optimal transfer trajectory.

Data Sources

  • NASA NeoWs — Near-Earth Object close-approach feed (365-day window)
  • JPL Small-Body Database — Orbital elements
  • Asterank — Asteroid mining value estimates
  • CesiumJS — 3D globe visualization
  • poliastro — Orbital mechanics library
